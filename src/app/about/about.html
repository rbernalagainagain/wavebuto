About Wavebuto

Wavebuto answers two questions: what is this, and how do I reach someone. Everything here serves one of the two. Anything that served neither was left out on purpose.

Every page is markup committed to the repository and rendered ahead of time. Nothing is assembled at runtime from somewhere else, and nothing loads from another origin — no fonts, no scripts, no embeds. The contact form makes the only request this site can make, and it goes to one place.

There are no accounts, no sessions, and no analytics. The site does not recognise you when you come back, because it never learned who you were. Your message is used to reply to you, and for nothing else.
