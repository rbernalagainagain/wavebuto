# Mission

Wavebuto is a tattoo artist's website. Its only purpose is to be the form people use to request a tattoo appointment with the
artist. The page shows an introduction and a video of the artist's work first, and then the form.

## Who it is for

- **People who already follow the artist**, for example on Instagram, and come to request an appointment.
- **People who don't know the artist yet** and find the site on their own.

## What the site does

- Collects one appointment request through one form: the person's contact details, the tattoo they want, reference images, their
  availability, and any other notes.
- Delivers each request to the artist **by email**.
- Speaks **English and Spanish**.

## What the site never does

- **No tracking.** No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking cookies.
- **No accounts.** Nobody signs up or logs in, and the site does not recognise returning visitors.
- **Nothing from third parties.** No script, font, stylesheet, image or embed is loaded from another domain.
- **Only what is needed.** The form asks for what the artist needs to answer a request, and nothing "just in case".
